Support

Found a bug, a word that doesn't belong, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Get in touch

Email bugjugapp@gmail.com. It helps to tell us your device, which stop on the map you were at, and what you expected to happen. If you use GitHub, you can also open an issue.

Common questions

What ages is it for?

Elementary school, roughly ages 6 to 11, and anyone else learning to touch-type. It starts from the home row, so no typing experience is needed, and every line is read aloud.

Does it need a real keyboard?

Yes, it's made for touch-typing. On Mac and Windows it just works. On an iPad or Android tablet, connect a Bluetooth or USB keyboard and the game starts. If you don't have one, a grown-up can allow the on-screen keys (from the grown-ups check on that screen, or the grown-ups page), but that won't teach touch-typing. It's laid out for US QWERTY keyboards.

How do I get to the grown-ups page?

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen or the town map) and choose Grown-ups. A grown-ups check asks you to press and hold a button for 3 seconds, then answer a quick question.

Can I turn off the voice, the finger guide or the motion?

Yes, all in Settings: sound and music volume, reading words aloud, the finger guide, less motion, full screen and word size.

My child is stuck on a level.

Every level has a Teach me how button that goes over its keys again, with a turn to type each one. There's no game over: if the Gridlock Meter fills up, a short home-row burst clears it and play carries on. A grown-up can also open every level for the session from the grown-ups page.

The game says Caps Lock is on.

Press Caps Lock once to turn it off. Capitals come in the last part of town, typed with Shift.

Can progress move to another device?

Not yet: each device, and each web browser, keeps its own progress.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tangletown Typing works fully offline.
