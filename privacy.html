Privacy policy

Tangletown Typing collects no data at all. Here are the details.

Effective October 21, 2026

Tangletown Typing is a touch-typing game for children. It is built to work entirely on your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal information, from children or anyone else, because none of it ever reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts or sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs. The game makes no network requests of its own.

What you type

Keystrokes are used only to play: they're checked against the word on screen and counted for speed, accuracy and which keys need practice. Nothing typed is recorded as text or sent anywhere.

Progress and settings

Stars, best laps, the ghost scooter, typing speed history, per-key accuracy, insects caught and settings are saved only on your device (in the browser's own storage when played on the web). The racing name is picked from a list of made-up nicknames; the game never asks for a real name.

Voices

Story and lesson lines are recordings that ship inside the game. Some words are read by your device's built-in speech voice, which Tangletown Typing uses without recording or sending anything. In a web browser, the browser chooses that voice; some browsers offer online voices that are provided by the browser maker under its own privacy policy.

BugJug codes

Insects caught in the game can be sent to BugJug, our bug-pet app, as a code shown on screen. A grown-up can save the code as a picture. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Purchases

If you buy Tangletown Typing, the store you buy it from (Steam, the App Store or Google Play) handles the payment. We never see your name or payment details.

Permissions

Tangletown Typing doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or internet. On iPad and Android it checks whether a hardware keyboard is connected, which stays on the device.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to review, share or delete. This is true for children under 13 (COPPA), and in every country.

Deleting your data

Deleting Tangletown Typing removes all of its data (on the web, clear the site's data in your browser). A grown-up can also reset progress from the grown-ups page.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
